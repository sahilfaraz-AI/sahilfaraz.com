Aug-Fice, a quiet office you can wear

A mixed reality focus app. It puts a virtual partition between you and a busy room, then runs your focus sets with breaks, or an untimed Zen session.

Checking this device.

Prepare your to-do list

Tasks stay in this browser. In the app, choose Import from web app on the to-do screen.

Three ways to interact
  • Hand ray. Point with your hand and pinch to select. You can also touch buttons with your index finger. Controllers work the same way.
  • Head tracking. Point with your head and pinch to tap. Switch to it in Settings.
  • Mouse and keyboard. Click to select and type task names. Drag the bar under the window to move it, or a corner bracket to resize it.
